# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in SatsVerify respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Be transparent about experience, test results, security claims, and limitations.
- Help maintain a collaborative environment for contributors and reviewers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Knowingly misrepresenting tests, security properties, or project capabilities.
- Abusive behavior in project spaces or during project-related events.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a verified contact method associated with GitHub account `Ugarba202`. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and spaces used for SatsVerify project activities.
